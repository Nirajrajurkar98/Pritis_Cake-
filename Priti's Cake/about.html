Our Sweet Story

Priti's Cake was born in 2016 from a simple dream — to bring joy to every celebration through beautifully crafted, delicious cakes. What started as a home kitchen passion project has grown into a beloved local bakery trusted by thousands of families.

Our founder, Priti, learned the art of baking from her grandmother and has spent years perfecting recipes that combine traditional flavors with modern designs. Every cake that leaves our kitchen carries her personal touch and dedication.

We believe that a great cake is more than just dessert — it's a memory, a celebration, a moment of pure joy. That's why we put our heart into every single creation.
