Our Story & Craft

Baked with Passion in Rahuri Since 2025

What began as a home kitchen passion project has grown into a cherished local bakery, bringing joy to celebrations across Rahuri.

Founded by Priti Chuttarwith recipes perfected over years, our bakery combines time-honored baking techniques with modern, elegant aesthetics. Every cake that leaves our kitchen is baked fresh to order using wholesome, locally sourced ingredients — no shortcuts and no compromises.
